HOUSE: THE GAME

HOMEPAGE

FRIDGE

You crack the fridge open with the crowbar. What do you expect to find inside? Old food? Cans of drink? Neither? Both? What could it possibly be?


A deli container with something inside. You tentatively open it and find a furry lump of something that looks suspiciously like a little paw. And some soil. The ghost spins around excitedly.
